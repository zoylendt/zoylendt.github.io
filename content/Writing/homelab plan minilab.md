## Drive cages

I'm only listing drive cages for 2,5" SATA drives. All cages fit in a 5,25" slot (unless noted otherwise).  
Maybe use a [ZimaBlade (83+€)](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B0D4D37ZVS) as storage server? It has an exposed PCIex4 port (and 2 SATA power ports with [this adapter cable](https://www.amazon.de/-/en/zimaboard-SATA-Y-Cable-ZimaBlade/dp/B0BQVYW9MC)!).
