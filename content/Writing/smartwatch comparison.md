Here I want to document my search for a new budget smartwatch. Since there is a plethora of different models available it should be best to first make clear what I expect from the device.

Currently I have:
  - Huawei Watch Fit (1st generation, modell `Stia-B09`, serial no. `LRBGA20819008052`), purchased 2020-10-14 via Amazon.de
    - needs a new band
    - currently retrying it
  - Amazfit GTS 2 Mini, purchased 2021-09-02 via Amazon.de for 90€
    - not longer used because of missing flashlight function
    - I have to retry it (now on iOS)!
  - Apple Watch 5 Aluminium Space Gray 44mm, purchased 2023-12-31 via kleinanzeigen.de (for 90€ incl. accessories)
    - horrendous battery life, like approx. 18h at best

Desired functions/specs (not sorted by importance):
  - long battery life (min. 4 days of typical use)
  - waterproof (shower/rain/swimming)
  - possibility to (automatically!) export collected data (e.g. to CSV)
  - compatibility with iOS
  - stand-alone capabilities (without a phone nearby)
    - GPS
    - data collection and later sync
    - music/Spotify storage & direct playback to bluetooth headphones
  - step count, heart rate, sleep tracking
  - flashlight function

ToDo: sort requirements by importance!

Central issue: Huawei apparently has by far the best budget smartwatches but a custom data export is nearly impossible

Candidates:
  - Huawei Watch Fit 3 (~110€)
  - Samsung Galaxy Fit3 (~45€)
  - Huawei Band 10 (~50€)
  - Amazfit Active 2 ([~100€](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B0DSPWKJW4))

Non-budget candidates:
  - Amazfit GTS 4 (~230)
  - Apple Watch SE (2022) (~350€)
